## Goal

Everything needed to launch in public and to show Minab live on TV without risk: a scripted demo that works with the network off, a recorded backup, screenshots, a case study, posts, a one-page fact sheet, and a rehearsal checklist. The owner records and posts; the session prepares everything else.

## Why now

The site is live (W4) and Minab is embeddable (V2). The launch itself is timed by the owner (D02).

## Read

- `playground/design/launch-kit.md` (storyboard, shot list, case study and post drafts)
- `playground/README.md` (routes, share links, embed)
- `docs/production/README.md` ("What production level means": the claims the material may make)
- Decisions D44, D40, D02

## Ask first

- The date and the channel of the TV appearance, and how long the slot is.
- Which language the live demo is in (English, Persian, or both).
- Who operates the demo live (the owner, or someone else who needs the script).

## Build

1. **Update `launch-kit.md`** for the designed site and today's features: add the Console tab (`LOG`), a Persian example, exact decimals, "runs in your browser and in NestJS".
2. **TV demo** (D44), in `playground/design/tv-demo.md`:
   - a **2-minute script**, minute by minute: what to click, what to type, what to say (in the chosen language or languages), what the audience sees;
   - a **demo mode** URL that preloads every asset (Monaco, PGlite, the demo dataset) on open, so nothing loads during the live part. Add it if the site does not have it: a query flag (for example `?demo=tv`) handled in **routes or hooks only if the contract allows**, otherwise ask the owner; it may also raise the font size for a TV screen (150–200%) and pick the high-contrast theme;
   - **offline check**: after the demo page is loaded, the whole script works with the network off (PGlite runs in the browser). Test it and write the result;
   - a **backup video** recording plan (the owner records it: same script, 1080p, captions);
   - a **rehearsal checklist**: device, browser version, display scaling, zoom, network off test, battery and power, notifications off, a second device with the backup video, the exact URLs on a card.
3. **Screenshots**: the shot list in `launch-kit.md`, taken from the live site (light and dark), stored where the owner wants (not in the repository if large).
4. **Case study** (portfolio): update the draft in `launch-kit.md` with the production story (correctness proof, runtime in two places, security, performance numbers from `docs/performance.md`).
5. **Fact sheet** (`playground/design/fact-sheet.md`): what Minab is in three sentences, who it is for, three numbers (for example: tests, budgets met, languages of names supported), links.
6. **Posts**: English drafts (and Persian if wanted) for the channels in D40.
7. **Uptime check**: a simple scheduled workflow (`site-uptime.yml`, every 10 minutes during launch week) that fetches the landing page and the `.wasm` file and fails if either is down; the owner gets a GitHub notification.

## Files

`playground/design/launch-kit.md`, `playground/design/tv-demo.md` (new), `playground/design/fact-sheet.md` (new), `.github/workflows/site-uptime.yml` (new), demo-mode code only where the design contract allows.

## Do not touch

Engine and content behavior.

## Output

A launch you can rehearse and a demo that cannot fail on stage.

## Test scenarios

- The full TV script runs twice in a row on the live site with the network off after load.
- The demo URL loads everything in under 10 seconds on a normal connection (measure; write the number).

## Done when

The script, demo mode, offline proof, checklists, fact sheet and post drafts are ready; the owner has rehearsed once and set the status to `done`.

## Not here

Shamsine's own TV demo (its plan, D44).

## Later

None expected.

## If it grows

Post drafts may move to Later. The TV script, demo mode and the offline proof must stay.
