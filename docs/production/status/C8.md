## Done
`ParentRecord`, `NameRef` and `Subquery` are now registered in the validator. A failure nested inside another expression is reported once, at the inner node (the `origin` mechanism from Phase 7 stops a second report by the parent). `ORDERBY` is defined and pinned per D18 (a).

- **Validator** (`src/language/minab-validator.ts`). Registers exactly `ParentRecord`, `NameRef` and `Subquery`; `Expression` is not registered. A new `checkNameRefTypeChecks` skips two kinds of `NameRef` that are not values: the callee of a call (the call reports an unknown function) and a `SELECT` alias used in `ORDERBY`.
- **Tests.** `test/nested-failures.test.ts` (new, 9 tests): nested `^`, nested unknown name (in an `IN` list and in a comparison), a broken subquery in a comparison, each with one diagnostic that points at the inner node. No-false-positive tests: valid subqueries and calls, an `ORDERBY` alias, `f(1)` with no diagnostic on `f`.
- **Query cases.** `test/queries/cases/c8-orderby.cases.ts`: `ORDERBY` on a column that `SELECT` does not list, and on a `SELECT` alias. Both run on PGlite.
- **Spec §4.1.** Item 7 now says `ORDERBY` takes a `SELECT` alias or any expression over the source row.
- **Changelog.** `changes/C8.md`.
